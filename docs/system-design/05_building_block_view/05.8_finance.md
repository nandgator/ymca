# 05.8 — Finance

Owns money: what is owed, by whom, to whom, what was charged, what was paid,
and how the two are reconciled.

Deliberately provider-agnostic. The domain records obligations and
settlements; adapters record what an external provider actually did; a
reconciliation step links them. No provider concept appears anywhere in the
domain model.

---

## 5.8.1 Responsibilities

**Owns**

- `Invoice` — an obligation between two parties
- `Charge` — a line on an invoice, with its components
- `Payment` — a settlement attempt and its outcome
- `Refund`, `Credit`, `Adjustment`
- `TaxTreatment` — jurisdictional calculation
- Reconciliation between domain records and provider records

**Does not own**

- What things cost (Programme, Membership own their price definitions)
- Whether a person may buy (Membership, Authorization)
- Provider state — held by adapters and reconciled, never authoritative

---

## 5.8.2 Parties are generic

The single most important structural decision here (ADR-082).

```txt
Invoice.payer  → Party
Invoice.payee  → Party

where Party = Person | Organization
```

```txt
WRONG    Invoice.member_id → Person
RIGHT    Invoice.payer_party_id → Party
```

Costs nothing now; a migration later. Every financial obligation in the
movement is between two parties on a schedule:

```txt
Person       → Association    membership fee, programme fee, hostel charge
Organization → Organization   affiliation dues, regional levies
Association  → Person         refund, stipend, honorarium
```

Inter-organizational dues are deferred (ADR-085), but arrive as a workflow
addition rather than a schema change. That is the entire point of paying the
modelling cost now.

```txt
Party
    id
    kind              PERSON | ORGANIZATION
    person_id         nullable → Person
    organization_id   nullable → Tenant | OrganizationalUnit
    tax_identifier    nullable
    billing_address_id nullable
```

## 5.8.3 Charge components

A charge is not a single amount (ADR-083).

```txt
Charge
    id
    invoice_id
    source_type        MEMBERSHIP | SUBSCRIPTION | ENROLLMENT |
                       BOOKING | STAY | DEPOSIT | DUES | ADJUSTMENT
    source_id
    description
    components []
```

```txt
ChargeComponent
    charge_id
    kind               ENTRANCE_FEE | SUBSCRIPTION_FEE | USAGE_FEE
                     | DEPOSIT | LATE_FEE | DISCOUNT | TAX
    recurrence         ONE_TIME | RECURRING
    amount             signed; discounts are negative
    currency
    tax_treatment_id   nullable
```

```txt
Membership admission — Association A
    ENTRANCE_FEE        ONE_TIME     ₹  500
    SUBSCRIPTION_FEE    RECURRING    ₹1,200
    TAX (GST 18%)                    ₹  306
                                     ───────
                                     ₹2,006
```

Associations charge a distinct entrance fee alongside the recurring
subscription, and state tax separately on listed prices. A single `amount`
field cannot express that a member pays the entrance fee once and the
subscription annually, which makes renewal arithmetic wrong from the first
year.

**Discounts are negative components**, not a modified base amount. Preserving
"₹1,500 less ₹300 early-bird" rather than "₹1,200" is what makes revenue
reporting and dispute handling possible.

## 5.8.4 Tax

```txt
TaxTreatment
    id
    jurisdiction        from Tenant.jurisdiction
    code                e.g. GST_18, VAT_STANDARD, EXEMPT
    rate
    inclusive           boolean; is the listed price tax-inclusive?
    valid_from
    valid_until
```

Tax is a **pluggable per-jurisdiction calculator** behind an interface
(ADR-084). The platform ships the interface and a small number of
implementations; it does not attempt a universal tax engine.

```txt
calculate_tax(charge_components, jurisdiction, party, at_date)
    → tax components
```

Three things make this genuinely hard and are why it is isolated:

```txt
Rates change              valid_from/until on the treatment, never
                          retroactive to issued invoices

Exemption varies          charitable status may exempt some charges
                          and not others, differently per jurisdiction

Inclusive vs exclusive    some associations list tax-inclusive prices,
                          others add it at checkout
```

An invoice snapshots its tax treatment at issue. A later rate change does not
alter an issued invoice.

## 5.8.5 Invoice

```txt
Invoice
    id
    tenant_id
    payer_party_id
    payee_party_id
    number                tenant-visible, gapless per tenant per year
    currency
    issued_at
    due_at
    state
    charges []
    total_amount          derived, stored for integrity
    tax_snapshot          the treatments applied at issue
```

### State machine

```txt
      DRAFT
        │ issue
        ▼
      ISSUED ──────────▶ PAID ──────▶ CLOSED
        │                  │
        │ overdue          │ refund
        ▼                  ▼
     OVERDUE           REFUNDED
        │
        │ written off / cancelled
        ▼
     VOIDED
```

`number` must be gapless within a tenant and financial year — a common
statutory requirement for receipts. This forces allocation at issue rather
than at creation, which is why `DRAFT` invoices carry no number.

**Issued invoices are immutable.** Corrections are credit notes or
adjustments, never edits. This is not fastidiousness; an edited invoice
breaks the audit trail that charitable accounting depends on.

## 5.8.6 Payment

```txt
Payment
    id
    invoice_id
    party_id             who paid; may differ from payer on the invoice
    method               CARD | UPI | BANK_TRANSFER | CASH | CHEQUE
                       | PROVIDER_HOSTED
    amount
    currency
    state
    provider_reference   nullable; opaque
    received_at
    recorded_by          for offline methods
```

### State machine

```txt
    INITIATED
        │
   ┌────┴────┐
   ▼         ▼
FAILED   AUTHORIZED
             │
             ▼
          SETTLED ──────▶ RECONCILED
             │
             ▼
          REVERSED
```

`CASH` and `CHEQUE` are first-class, not afterthoughts. A substantial share
of associations take payment at a counter, and a model assuming online
settlement is unusable for them. Offline payments go straight to `SETTLED`
with `recorded_by` naming the staff member — which makes the audit trail the
control, since there is no provider to confirm.

**Partial payment is supported.** An invoice may have several payments; its
state derives from the sum, not from any single payment.

## 5.8.7 The provider facade

```txt
DOMAIN                        ADAPTER                    PROVIDER
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Invoice                       ProviderIntent
Payment          ◀────────▶   ProviderTransaction  ◀──▶  (external)
Refund                        ProviderRefund
```

```txt
PaymentPort            initiate, capture, refund, query
WebhookPort            receive and verify provider callbacks
ReconciliationPort     fetch settlement reports
```

### Rules

```txt
1  The domain never holds provider identifiers as meaningful values.
   provider_reference is opaque and used only for reconciliation.

2  The adapter never writes domain state directly.
   It records what the provider said; a reconciliation step
   decides what that means.

3  A provider may be swapped per tenant, per region, or per method
   without any domain change.

4  Webhooks are untrusted input. Every callback is verified,
   idempotent, and reconciled against a query to the provider —
   never accepted at face value.
```

Regional providers are the norm rather than the exception for a global
platform, and a facade is the only way to support several without the domain
learning any of their vocabularies.

### Reconciliation

```txt
ReconciliationRun
    id
    tenant_id
    provider_code
    period
    domain_total
    provider_total
    discrepancies []
    state           RUNNING | BALANCED | DISCREPANT | RESOLVED
```

`DISCREPANT` is an operational alert, not an error to swallow. The domain and
the provider disagreeing about money is exactly the condition that must
surface loudly.

## 5.8.8 Arrears

Finance detects; Membership decides (ADR-047).

```txt
Finance                    invoice OVERDUE beyond
                           SUSPENSION_THRESHOLD_DAYS  [Policy]
                                    ↓
                           emits ArrearsThresholdReached
                                    ↓
Membership                 policy-driven automation or a human
                           ISSUES a MembershipSuspension
                                    ↓
Authorization              entitlement resolution sees the suspension
```

Finance **never** suspends anything. It raises an event. This keeps the rule
from ADR-047 intact: authorization must never depend on live financial state,
and a suspension is always a recorded act with an actor.

The threshold is a policy, because associations differ — one treats a fee in
arrears for a month as ending entitlement to privileges; another allows a
longer grace period.

## 5.8.9 Refunds and deposits

```txt
Refund
    id
    payment_id
    amount
    reason_category   CANCELLATION | OVERPAYMENT | GOODWILL |
                      DEPOSIT_RETURN | ADMINISTRATIVE
    approved_by
    state
```

Refunds require authorization at a scope — `finance.refund` — and above a
policy-configured amount may require a `DecisionRecord`. A refund is a
transfer of association funds and should not be a single person's unlogged
discretion.

**Deposits** (05.5.6) are charge components of kind `DEPOSIT`, held against a
stay and settled at checkout:

```txt
DEPOSIT_SETTLED
    → full return         Refund(DEPOSIT_RETURN)
    → partial forfeiture  Refund(partial) + Charge(damage/adjustment)
    → full forfeiture     no refund; the deposit becomes revenue
```

The forfeiture rules themselves are tenant policy and are not yet specified —
see open items.

## 5.8.10 Published events

```txt
InvoiceIssued                → notification
InvoicePaid                  → Membership, Programme, Resource
InvoiceOverdue               → notification
ArrearsThresholdReached      → Membership
PaymentSettled               → the originating context
PaymentReversed              → the originating context, high-severity
RefundIssued                 → notification, audit
ReconciliationDiscrepant     → operational alert
```

`InvoicePaid` is what releases a `MembershipApplication` from `APPROVED` to
`ADMITTED`, and what confirms a `PENDING` enrollment or booking. Payment is
the gate, not the trigger — the approval already happened.

## 5.8.11 Authorization model fragment

```txt
type invoice
  relations
    define tenant: [tenant]
    define payer: [person, organizational_unit]
    define viewer: payer or finance_reader from tenant
```

Deliberately minimal. Financial *permissions* — who may issue, refund, write
off — are ordinary scoped role assignments, not special financial relations.
Invoice contents are business state and are never expressed as tuples.

A person may always view their own invoices via `payer`. Everything else
requires a role.

## 5.8.13 Open items

| Item | Note |
|---|---|
| Deposit forfeiture rules | The settlement step exists; partial forfeiture criteria are tenant policy and unspecified |
| Multi-currency | Each invoice has one currency; a tenant operating across currencies is untested |
| Instalments | Not modelled; a plan paid in three parts is currently three invoices |
| Accounting export | No ledger integration designed; associations will have existing accounting systems |
| Dues | Deferred (ADR-085); the seam is `AffiliationStateChanged` from 05.1 |
| Gapless numbering | Required, but the concurrency-safe allocation mechanism is not specified |
