# 05.3 — Membership

Owns the relationship between a person and an association: plans, admission,
lifecycle, and the two independent things a membership confers.

The largest subsection, because this is where the movement's practices
diverge most sharply and where the platform must therefore be most careful to
ship machinery rather than answers.

---

## 5.3.1 Responsibilities

**Owns**

- `MembershipPlan` — tenant-local plan definitions
- `MembershipApplication` — the admission workflow
- `Membership` — the relationship itself, with lifecycle
- `MembershipDependent` — coverage of additional people
- `Entitlement` — what a membership grants, on two axes
- `Subscription` — additional entitlements purchased on top

**Does not own**

- The person (Identity), the money (Finance), the facilities (Resource),
  the offerings (Programme), or the eligibility rules themselves (Policy)

---

## 5.3.2 Why plans are tenant-local

Three associations within one country, in one movement:

```txt
Association A   Junior, Student, Associate, Metropolitan, Life
Association B   Life, Married Couple Life, General, Student
Association C   Associate (a single plan)
```

They differ in categories, age bands, duration, whether facilities are
bundled, whether a joint membership exists, and whether membership is bought
or conferred. No global catalogue is possible, and a shipped default would fit
almost nobody (ADR-081).

The platform therefore defines **plan machinery**: eligibility criteria,
entitlement bundles, duration, acquisition mode, pricing hooks. Tenants
populate it.

```txt
MembershipPlan
    id
    tenant_id
    code                      tenant's own identifier
    name
    acquisition               PURCHASED | CONFERRED
    duration                  ANNUAL | FIXED_TERM | LIFETIME
    duration_months           nullable
    max_dependents            0 for individual plans
    eligibility_policy_id     → Policy (05.7)
    entitlement_bundle_id     → EntitlementBundle
    governance_profile_id     nullable → GovernanceProfile
    status                    DRAFT | OPEN | CLOSED_TO_NEW | RETIRED
    superseded_by_id          nullable
```

### Plans are versioned, never edited in place

`status` plus `superseded_by_id` exist because a plan is a commercial and
sometimes constitutional artifact. Changing the entitlements of a plan people
already hold is a silent bulk change to what they bought.

```txt
Editing a live plan          prohibited
Creating a successor plan    supported
Migrating holders            explicit, per-membership, auditable
```

`CLOSED_TO_NEW` is the common real state: existing life members keep a plan
that no longer accepts applicants.

### Acquisition mode

```txt
PURCHASED    the applicant pays, subject to approval
CONFERRED    a governing body elects the person, typically against
             a donation to the corpus (ADR-050)
```

Conferred plans skip the payment path entirely and require a
`DecisionRecord` from Governance (05.6) rather than an invoice. Modelling
Life membership as a purchase with an unusual price would misrepresent both
the constitutional act and the accounting.

## 5.3.3 The two entitlement axes

A membership confers two independent things (ADR-051).

```txt
Membership
 ├── FACILITY ENTITLEMENT     which resources and programmes
 │                            governed by the fee schedule
 │
 └── GOVERNANCE RIGHTS        vote, stand for election, hold office
                              governed by the constitution
```

They vary independently and are set by different instruments. A member may
hold maximal facility access and no vote; another may vote while using
nothing.

### Facility entitlement

```txt
EntitlementBundle
    id
    tenant_id
    name

EntitlementGrant
    bundle_id
    target_type       RESOURCE_TYPE | RESOURCE | PROGRAMME | ORG_UNIT
    target_id
    access_level      USE | BOOK | PRIORITY_BOOK
```

Bundling is entirely tenant-local and has no global default. One association
includes pool and health club in base membership; another requires a separate
subscription for the same facilities. Both are expressed as bundle contents.

```txt
Association A base bundle:  library, table tennis
                            (pool, gym via Subscription)

Association B base bundle:  pool, health club, indoor sports,
                            room and hall booking for guests
```

The scope of a broad plan is **within one tenant** (ADR-044). A plan granting
access at all branches, departments and centres grants it across that
association's units — not across the movement.

### Governance rights

```txt
GovernanceProfile
    id
    tenant_id
    may_vote
    may_stand_for_election
    may_hold_office
    eligibility_policy_id     → Policy
```

Kept separate from the entitlement bundle because the two are authored by
different bodies and change on different cycles — a fee schedule is revised
annually by management; a constitution is amended rarely by a general body.

**Data-protection note.** Constitutional eligibility criteria may reference
special-category personal data. The platform ships generic, tenant-configured
criteria evaluated through Policy and evidenced through Verification. It
hard-codes no constitutional rule and stores no special-category attribute on
the Person. Tenants encode their own constitutions and own the resulting
obligations.

## 5.3.4 Admission is a workflow, not a purchase

Membership is *admitted*, not bought (ADR-046). Associations reserve the
right to reject an application without assigning a reason, to require the
applicant to appear before a committee, and to terminate membership for
conduct prejudicial to the association.

```txt
MembershipApplication
    id
    tenant_id
    person_id
    plan_id
    submitted_at
    state
    decided_by_record_id     nullable → DecisionRecord (05.6)
    decided_at
    rejection_recorded       boolean; no reason field, by design
```

### State machine

```txt
        DRAFT
          │ submit
          ▼
      SUBMITTED
          │
          ▼
    UNDER_REVIEW ──────────┐
          │                │ interview required
          │                ▼
          │          INTERVIEW_SCHEDULED
          │                │
          │◀───────────────┘
          │
    ┌─────┴─────┐
    ▼           ▼
APPROVED    REJECTED
    │
    │ payment settled (PURCHASED plans)
    ▼
 ADMITTED ──────────▶ creates Membership
```

**`rejection_recorded` has no reason field.** This is deliberate. The
governing right is to reject without assigning a reason; providing a reason
field invites recording one, which creates a record the association never
intended to make and may not lawfully hold. Where a tenant chooses to record
reasons, it does so through Governance as a `DecisionRecord`, under that
context's access controls.

**An applicant is a Person with a pending relationship.** They need limited
platform access before becoming a member — to complete forms, upload
verification, pay. This is a distinct authorization state, not a membership.

### Approval authority resolves to real people

```txt
approve(application)
    requires permission membership.approve
    at scope: the tenant or an org unit above the application
    held via an Office (05.6) or a Role assignment
```

Whether that is a board, a committee or a general secretary is tenant
governance. The platform routes to whoever holds the permission and records
who exercised it.

## 5.3.5 Membership

```txt
Membership
    id
    tenant_id
    person_id                 the primary holder
    plan_id
    number                    tenant-visible identifier
    state
    admitted_at
    valid_from
    valid_until               null for LIFETIME
    application_id            → MembershipApplication
    conferred_by_record_id    nullable → DecisionRecord
```

### State machine

```txt
                  ACTIVE
                 ╱  │   ╲
      suspend   ╱   │    ╲  term ends
               ╱    │     ╲
       SUSPENDED    │     LAPSED
            │       │        │
    reinstate│      │        │ renew
            ╲       │       ╱
             ╲      │      ╱
              ▶  ACTIVE ◀─┘
                    │
                    │ terminate
                    ▼
              TERMINATED        terminal
```

**One active membership per tenant** is the normal rule (ADR-043). Five
programme subscriptions do not make five memberships. Concurrent memberships
in different tenants are permitted but rare.

### Suspension is explicit, never derived

```txt
MembershipSuspension
    id
    membership_id
    reason_category      ARREARS | CONDUCT | SAFEGUARDING | ADMINISTRATIVE
    issued_by            actor, never "system" without a delegating policy
    issued_at
    effective_from
    lifted_at
```

Arrears do not silently compute into lost entitlement (ADR-047). Automation
detects the condition and *issues* a suspension; the suspension is a recorded
fact with an actor and a timestamp.

This matters beyond tidiness. Authorization must never depend on live
financial state — an entitlement check cannot ask "is this invoice 31 days
overdue." It asks whether a suspension exists.

```txt
Detection            Finance raises ArrearsThresholdReached
Decision             policy-driven automation or a human issues suspension
Effect               entitlement resolution sees the suspension
Audit                one row, one actor, one reason category
```

### Age bands do not auto-transition

A junior member turning 19 is not silently re-planned or re-priced
(ADR-048). Eligibility is evaluated at application and at renewal. The
renewal path surfaces the mismatch and requires a decision.

## 5.3.6 Dependents

```txt
MembershipDependent
    id
    membership_id
    person_id
    relationship        SPOUSE | CHILD | OTHER
    added_at
    removed_at
```

One accountable primary holder, zero or more dependents (ADR-045). Chosen
over multi-holder because it keeps fee accountability unambiguous and
generalizes from couple memberships to family and corporate cases.

Dependents inherit the membership's facility entitlement. They do **not**
inherit governance rights — voting is personal, and a plan covering two
people does not thereby create two votes unless the tenant's governance
profile says so.

**Not guardianship.** A dependent may be an adult spouse. A guardian may hold
no membership at all. See 05.2.4.

## 5.3.7 Subscriptions

Additional entitlements purchased on top of a membership (ADR-057).

```txt
Subscription
    id
    membership_id
    entitlement_bundle_id
    state               ACTIVE | SUSPENDED | LAPSED | CANCELLED
    valid_from
    valid_until
```

```txt
Alice ──holds──> Membership(base) ──grants──> library, table tennis
Alice ──holds──> Subscription(pool) ──grants──> pool
```

A subscription is a relationship carrying an entitlement bundle, structurally
identical to the membership's own bundle. This keeps entitlement resolution
uniform: gather all bundles the person holds, union their grants.

---

## 5.3.8 Entitlement resolution

The read path other contexts depend on. Note carefully what it does and does
not answer.

```txt
resolve_entitlements(person, tenant) → set of EntitlementGrant

  1. active Membership in tenant?           → its bundle
  2. active Subscriptions on it?            → their bundles
  3. dependent on someone's Membership?     → that bundle
  4. cross-tenant recognition?              → deferred (ADR-052)

  minus: anything suspended
```

```txt
ANSWERS       is Alice entitled to use the pool at all?
DOES NOT      may Alice swim at 18:00 on Tuesday?
```

The second question belongs to Resource (05.5) and depends on windows,
capacity and booking state. Principle 3, applied at this seam.

**Step 4 exists deliberately.** Foreign membership recognition is not built,
but resolution routes through a resolver capable of consulting grants
originating outside the tenant. Reciprocity later becomes a new grant source,
not a re-architecture.

## 5.3.9 Published events

```txt
MembershipApplicationSubmitted   → Governance, Finance
MembershipApplicationApproved    → Finance
MembershipAdmitted               → Authorization, Finance
MembershipSuspended              → Authorization  (synchronous)
MembershipReinstated             → Authorization
MembershipLapsed                 → Authorization  (synchronous)
MembershipTerminated             → Authorization  (synchronous)
DependentAdded                   → Authorization
DependentRemoved                 → Authorization  (synchronous)
SubscriptionActivated            → Authorization
SubscriptionLapsed               → Authorization  (synchronous)
GovernanceRightsChanged          → Governance
```

Every removal is synchronous. Every addition may lag.

## 5.3.10 Authorization model fragment

```txt
type membership
  relations
    define tenant: [tenant]
    define holder: [person]
    define dependent: [person]
    define covered: holder or dependent

type entitlement_bundle
  relations
    define granted_by: [membership, subscription]
    define beneficiary: covered from granted_by

type resource
  relations
    define entitled: beneficiary from entitling_bundle
```

Suspension is **not** modelled as an FGA relation. It is domain state,
consulted after the graph answers. Putting suspension in the graph would
require the authorization store to track financial and disciplinary state —
exactly the coupling Principle 3 exists to prevent.
